Engineered for Precision

Step 1: User Query

User inputs specific topics, keywords, or news headlines to index.

Step 2: Story Discovery

System performs cross-portal crawling and scraper retrieval of related coverage.

Step 3: Source Identification

Outlets are identified and matched against our verified registry databases.

Step 4: Sequence Analysis

Timestamps are parsed and normalized to sequence publishing order exactly.

Step 5: Evidence Scoring

Deduplication algorithm calculates cosine similarities to detect repetition.

A score is a signal, not a verdict.

Our algorithm processes quantitative patterns in journalism. It does not possess the editorial intuition of a human editor, but it provides the maps and source history analysts need to work faster and detect media bias.
