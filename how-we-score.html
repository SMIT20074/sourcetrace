What Lowers Confidence

  • visibility_off
    Anonymity Lack of clear authorship or untraceable original publication source.
  • history
    Lack of Supporting Records Claims that exist in a digital vacuum without contextual archives or corroborative records.
  • error
    Contradictory Data Major discrepancies in logistical details (times, locations, participants) between syndicated outlets.

Temporal Reliability

Confidence isn't static. As new evidence emerges or old records are archived, our scores evolve. A "92%" today may adjust as the context around a source deepens over months or years.
